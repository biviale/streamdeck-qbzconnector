Checking the session…

The plugin uses its own Qobuz session: QBZ encrypts its own, which cannot be reused. The token is kept in the system keyring (KWallet), never in OpenDeck's configuration.

Qobuz has no notion of a dislike: this key adds or removes favourites.

tint from the cover

Global setting: it applies to every QBZ Connector key. The cover's dominant colour is darkened as much as it takes for the symbols to stay readable.
